About Us

Leading autonomous test services in Hillsboro, Missouri

Autonomous Test Company delivers comprehensive testing and validation solutions for autonomous vehicles, drones, and robotic systems. Based in Hillsboro, MO, we serve clients across the Midwest with state-of-the-art facilities and expert engineering teams.

We are committed to ensuring the safety, reliability, and performance of autonomous systems through rigorous testing, simulation, and data analysis. Our team brings decades of experience in robotics, AI, and automotive engineering.
